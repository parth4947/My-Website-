CPM

Impression buying, where reach is the goal.

  • CPM / CPC: click or impression buying, where it fits the goal.
  • CPI: cost per install.
  • CPR: cost per registration.
  • CPA: cost per in-app action, such as a deposit, trade or purchase.
  • CPS: cost per sale.
  • Rev Share: a share of the revenue your users generate.
